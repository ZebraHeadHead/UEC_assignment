AI Settings

Each group always gets a local recap at the end, computed in this page; no data leaves your computer. With a Claude API key, opening a week (sidebar, weeks table or breadcrumb) has Claude label every event of that week High / Medium / Low (model ) and then review the High-risk ones at the top of the page (model ). Weeks you haven't opened stay “Unrated”.

When a week is labelled, its events are sent to Anthropic in batches (up to 8 field changes per event, values cut to 40 characters); the review then sends up to 60 of the week's High events. Values are truncated, and passwords, secrets, keys and bank/account numbers are redacted. The key is stored only in this browser (localStorage). Don't host this page publicly with a key saved.
